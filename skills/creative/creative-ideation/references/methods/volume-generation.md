# Volume Generation

**One line:** Quantity first — set a quota, defer all judgment, and mine the long tail where the non-obvious ideas live.

**Invented by:** The "defer judgment / quantity breeds quality" principles of Alex Osborn's brainstorming (*Applied Imagination*, 1953); reinforced by Dean Keith Simonton's research showing the most creative producers are also the most *prolific* (equal-odds rule).

## When to use

- Phase = GENERATING and you need many options fast.
- The first few ideas are predictable and you suspect the good one is further down the list.
- As a warm-up before a harder, more targeted method.

## The idea

Idea quality is roughly a hit-rate on volume: the people who make the most great things also make the most things, period. The first ideas you produce cluster at the obvious center; usable novelty disproportionately appears **after** you've exhausted the easy ones (ideas ~30–100 on a long list). Separating *generation* from *evaluation* is essential — judging while generating strangles the tail before it appears.

## The method

1. **Set a hard quota.** "50 ideas" (or 100 on slop terrain). The number matters — it licenses bad ideas and keeps you going past the obvious.
2. **Defer judgment completely.** No "that's dumb" during generation. Write everything, including jokes, impossibilities, duplicates.
3. **Build on your own list.** Each idea can spawn a variant. Momentum beats quality early.
4. **Push through the wall.** Around idea 20–30 it gets hard — that wall is the point; the fresh stuff is just past it.
5. **Then switch hats and harvest.** Only now evaluate. Mark the 3–5 that clear the `references/anti-slop.md` bar. They're usually in the back half.

## Worked example

Prompt: "ideas for a conference badge."
- Ideas 1–10: QR code, NFC, color by track… (center, discard).
- Ideas 25–40 (post-wall): a badge that's a working kazoo; a badge whose lanyard length signals how much you want to be approached; a badge that's blank until someone you've met signs it; a seed-paper badge you plant afterward.
- Harvest: the "approachability lanyard" (social mechanism, cheap to prototype) is the grounded non-obvious pick.

## Output shape

You don't have to show all 50, but show that you went past the wall — list the handful of obvious ones you discarded, then develop the tail finds with mechanism + failure mode.

## Pairs well with

`scamper` (mutate the pile), any targeted method afterward (volume to warm up, then route for depth).
