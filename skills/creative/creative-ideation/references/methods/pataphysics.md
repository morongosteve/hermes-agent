# Pataphysics

**One line:** The science of imaginary solutions; take the exception as the law and build a rigorous system around it.

**Invented by:** Alfred Jarry (*Exploits and Opinions of Dr. Faustroll, Pataphysician*, 1911). Later touchstones: the Oulipo, Raymond Queneau, the College of 'Pataphysics.

## When to use

- You want *genuine* strangeness with internal logic, not mere randomness.
- `lateral-provocations` gave you weird but shapeless; pataphysics gives weird-with-a-system.
- The brief rewards a fully committed invented world (speculative design, art, fiction, a conceptual product).

## The idea

Where science generalizes from the regular case, pataphysics generalizes from the **exception**. Every particular is its own law. You invent a solution to a problem that may not exist, and then take it *completely seriously* — the humor and the depth both come from rigor applied to an absurd premise.

## The method

1. **Pick an exception and promote it to a law.** Not "most cups hold liquid" but "this one cup, which rings at middle C — what world is lawful for *it*?"
2. **Invent the imaginary solution.** Describe a device/practice/institution that solves a problem nobody has, as if it obviously should exist.
3. **Be rigorous inside the fiction.** Give it units, rules, edge cases, a history. The discipline is what separates pataphysics from whimsy.
4. **Report deadpan.** Present it as established fact. No winking.

## Worked example

Prompt: "something strange about commuting."

- Exception promoted to law: *the subway car is a single slow musical instrument; each rider's grip on a pole is a fret.*
- Imaginary solution: a transit authority that tunes carriages, publishes the daily "line chord", and schedules trains so the whole system plays a 24-hour composition no single rider ever hears in full.
- Rigor: define the tuning (A-train in A minor), the transposition at interchanges, what a delay does to the harmony.
- Grounded spin-off (the buildable corner): a real web toy that sonifies live transit-arrival data into an ambient drone — one API, one `<audio>` graph.

## Output shape

Present the invented system as fact, with at least one concrete rule and one edge case. Then offer the grounded spin-off so the user has something to actually make.

## Pairs well with

`oulipo` (constraint as the system's law), `defamiliarization` (the deadpan reporting voice).
