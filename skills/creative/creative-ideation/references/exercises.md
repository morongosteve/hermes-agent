# Exercises — Time-Boxed Creative Drills

Hand these to a user who wants to *practice* ideation rather than get ideas for a specific project, or run one yourself to warm up before a hard generative task. Grouped by the time they take. Each names the method it trains.

## 5 minutes

- **Ten uses** (volume-generation): pick an object in the room; list 10 uses that are not its intended use. The last 3 are the point.
- **One card** (oblique-strategies): draw one strategy ("Honor thy error as a hidden intention", "Use an old idea", "What would your closest friend do?") and apply it to whatever you're stuck on. One move only.
- **Wrong on purpose** (lateral-provocations): state the most false thing you can about your problem ("the user never opens the app"), then harvest one real idea from it.
- **N+7 a sentence** (oulipo): take any sentence, replace each noun with the 7th noun after it in a dictionary. Read the result aloud.

## 30 minutes

- **Refuse five** (anti-slop): on a high-slop prompt (a wellness app, an AI tool), write the five most obvious ideas, cross them out, then generate five from further out.
- **SCAMPER a product** (scamper): take something you use daily; run all seven operations; keep the two strangest results.
- **Defamiliarize** (defamiliarization): describe a mundane routine (making coffee) as if you'd never seen it and don't know the words for the objects.
- **Blend two** (analogy-and-blending): pick two unrelated things (a library + a nightclub); design the thing that is genuinely both.

## 1 hour

- **Constraint poem** (oulipo): write 12 lines under a hard formal constraint (a lipogram in E, or a snowball where each word is one letter longer).
- **Premortem** (premortem-and-inversion): take a plan you believe in; write the news story explaining why it failed; fix the top two causes.
- **First-principles teardown** (first-principles): take an industry norm ("resumes", "the 9-5", "textbooks"); strip to what's actually required; rebuild.
- **TRIZ a contradiction** (triz-principles): name one real "if I improve X, Y gets worse" in your work; walk the 40 principles; find one resolution.

## A day

- **Dérive** (derive-and-mapping): spend a few hours wandering a space (a city, a codebase, a research field) with no goal; map what pulled your attention; let the map suggest the project.
- **Affinity sort** (affinity-diagrams): dump 50+ raw observations onto cards; cluster bottom-up; name the themes that emerge; write the one insight.
- **Volume day** (volume-generation): generate 100 ideas on one prompt. The first 30 are warm-up; the gold is after 70.

## A week

- **Habit spine** (creative-discipline): pick a daily creative ritual (Tharp's box); fill the box each day with raw material; on day 7 find the single spine that organizes it.
- **Compression hunt** (compression-progress): keep a log of things that surprised you; at week's end pick the one whose explanation would compress the most of the rest — that's your research question.
- **Ship one** (any): take the single best idea from any exercise above and build the smallest real version. An idea that ships teaches more than ten that don't.
