# Heuristics — Extended Decision Tree

Edge cases and judgment calls the main `SKILL.md` routing table doesn't fully cover. Read this when the route isn't obvious.

## Reading mood signals

The user's *affect* often overrides their stated domain. Words that flip you to `lateral-provocations` / `pataphysics` regardless of domain:

- "weird", "strange", "surprising", "unexpected", "wild", "out there"
- "less obvious", "too safe", "too normal", "boring", "predictable"
- "make it interesting", "blow my mind", "something I haven't seen"

A flat, task-like tone ("I need three options for the landing page headline") means *stay practical* — route by domain, keep the grounded idea central.

## Stacking two methods (do it explicitly)

When signals genuinely conflict, name both methods and say how they combine:

- **"weird startup ideas"** → `jobs-to-be-done` (frame the real job) + `lateral-provocations` (break the obvious product shape). Generate the job first, then provoke the solution.
- **"a poem about climate grief that isn't clichéd"** → `oulipo` (a constraint to force fresh language) + `defamiliarization` (make the familiar subject strange).
- **"rethink our onboarding from scratch"** → `first-principles` (strip assumptions) + `leverage-points` (find where a small change moves the whole system).

Rule: stack at most two. Three methods is noise.

## When to ask vs. when to decide

Ask ONE clarifying question only when the answer changes the *method*, not the content:

- Ambiguous phase: "Are you generating new ideas, or choosing among ones you already have?"
- Ambiguous domain: "Is this for fiction, an essay, or copy?"

Do NOT ask when you can route on the dominant signal and proceed — a good idea set beats a clarifying question the user has to stop and answer.

## Repeated / returning users

- **Same question again** → switch methods. A different method samples a different region of idea-space; repeating the method repeats the distribution.
- **"More"** after a set → `scamper` or `volume-generation` on the set, or push one idea further rather than widening.
- **"None of these"** → you were probably at center. Jump to `lateral-provocations`, refuse five, come back stranger.

## Domain-edge calls

- **Game design** → ARTIFACT (mechanics = `triz`/`first-principles`) or OBJECT (feel = `oblique-strategies`) depending on whether they're stuck on *system* or *vibe*.
- **A name / title / tagline** → `oulipo` (constraint) or `chance-and-remix` (recombine), not constraint-dispatch.
- **"A business idea"** with no vertical → high-slop; `jobs-to-be-done` + refuse five, or `analogy-and-blending` (port a model from another industry).
- **Research question** → `compression-progress` to pick *what's worth asking*; `polya` to attack one already chosen.

## Anti-patterns (you're doing it wrong if…)

- You produced a bare numbered list with no named method.
- Every idea is equally reasonable and equally forgettable.
- You picked the fanciest method to look clever instead of the fitting one.
- You kept generating after the user chose an idea (rule 7 — build it instead).
- All your ideas are weird and none is buildable (or vice versa).
- You narrated four routing steps and then gave shallow ideas. Depth per idea > visible routing.
